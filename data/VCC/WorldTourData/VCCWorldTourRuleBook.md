# 1. League Overview

## 1.1 Golden Order

The Golden Order consists of 10 teams. The regular season lasts eight weeks, and every team plays each other once. The top four teams advance to the playoffs.

Standings and playoff seeding are decided in this order:

1. Record
2. Head-to-head result
3. Strength of victory
4. Bingos

The first seed plays the fourth seed, and the second seed plays the third seed in the first round. The winners advance to the championship; the other teams play a third-place match. All playoff matches are best-of-three sets.

## 1.2 Crucible

The Crucible consists of 12 teams across four divisions: A, B, C, and D. The regular season lasts eight weeks. Each team plays every team in its own division and every team in two other divisions.

The winner of each division and the two non-division winners with the best records advance to the playoffs. Division winners receive seeds 1 through 4, and the two non-division winners receive seeds 5 and 6.

Standings are decided in this order:

1. Record
2. Head-to-head result
3. Strength of schedule
4. Strength of victory
5. Bingos

The first and second seeds receive playoff byes. Playoff sets are best-of-three.

## 1.3 Reporting Results

After a match, upload a screenshot of the completed board to the Bingo Results section of the Ignite Discord. Use the correct tag on the post.

## 1.4 Prize Money

The Golden Order has at least **$600 USD** in prize money provided by HooZher:

| Finish | Prize |
| --- | ---: |
| First | $300 |
| Second | $200 |
| Third | $100 |

Donations may increase the prize pool, but additional donations are not guaranteed.

## 1.5 Referees

HooZher assigns referees for Golden Order matches and all commentated matches.

Referees are encouraged but not required for Crucible matches. Players who want a referee are responsible for first asking the community. If no community referee is available, contact HooZher directly and he will find one.

## 1.6 Scheduling

Golden Order matches are scheduled with HooZher. Crucible matches may be played at any mutually agreed time during the assigned week.

- Schedule Crucible games in the text channel created for the matchup.
- Tag HooZher and Jay once the match is scheduled.
- A team that does not arrive within 15 minutes of the agreed time forfeits the match.
- If negligence prevents scheduling, the negligent team receives a loss.
- If both teams are negligent, both teams receive a loss.
- Contact HooZher or Jay immediately about scheduling disputes.

If a player is unable to schedule or arrive on time for two Ignite league matches within six months, HooZher may remove that player from current and future Elden Squares events.

# 2. Streaming and Setup

## 2.1 Required Mods and Tools

Matches are hosted through the **EldenBingo application**. Players must use the **Athena Randomizer** to randomize weapons and play on the current Elden Ring patch.

If Elden Ring is patched during the season, league organizers will announce how matches should proceed.

Allowed tools and modifications include:

- ERTool for stutter fixes, avoiding achievement bugs or freezing, and skipping the intro
- SkipTheIntro
- Elden Ring Map Genie
- Fextralife
- Rune-level calculators

Players may not seek help from other people, including Twitch chat.

## 2.2 Streaming Requirements

Commentated matches must be streamed live on Twitch.

- Give HooZher your Twitch channel before the match.
- Contact HooZher if streaming is a problem so an alternative can be arranged.
- Use the overlay supplied by HooZher.
- Players may place anything they want in the overlay margins.
- If using a facecam, align it with the indicated position so it can appear on the main broadcast.
- Out-of-game audio is prohibited during league matches, except for the player's microphone.

## 2.3 Chat Moderation

Players may not receive spoilers, hints, or other match information from Twitch chat.

Streamers must ensure that moderators promptly remove prohibited information. If chat reveals match information, responsibility rests with the player or streamer. Receiving prohibited information may result in penalties, including match forfeiture.

# 3. Match Structure

## 3.1 Starting on Time

Matches begin at the agreed time. A player who cannot begin because of a personal or technical issue must tell the opponent.

A player who does not arrive and provides no communication receives a 15-minute grace period. If the player has not arrived when that period ends, the player forfeits the match.

## 3.2 Three-Minute Warmup

Each match begins with a three-minute warmup. Players may see the board during this period but may not control their characters.

Before the warmup begins, players or referees confirm that the weapon randomizer works correctly. Players may not inspect a starting class other than Vagabond before this confirmation.

During the warmup, players may:

- Select a starting class and keepsake
- Check Roundtable Hold
- Purchase items
- Perform other setup that does not leave Roundtable Hold or begin Fia's quest

Players may leave Roundtable Hold or begin Fia's quest once the timer reaches zero.

A false start results in a time penalty approximately twice the advantage gained. When a referee is present, the referee determines the official penalty length.

## 3.3 Match Duration

The official match timer begins when the warmup ends. A match lasts no more than two hours. If no victory condition is met within two hours, the match ends on points or proceeds to overtime under the victory rules below.

## 3.4 Victory Conditions

Every match must have a winner.

- The first team to **13 points** wins.
- Each marked square is worth **1 point**.
- Each completed Bingo line is worth **2 points**.

If the teams are tied after two hours, they enter overtime for up to 15 minutes. The first team to mark a square during overtime wins. If no square is marked before overtime expires, the team that first reached the tying score wins.

# 4. Skips and Cheeses

## 4.1 General Standard

The league is intended to be played without major skips or cheeses. A skip or cheese is banned when it intentionally performs an action that is clearly unintended to a knowledgeable player and involves any of the following:

1. Gravity kills
2. Stake skips
3. Directly skipping an otherwise required boss
4. Breaking enemy AI so it has no meaningful opportunity to damage the player
5. Fall-damage canceling
6. Running bosses into kill planes
7. Clipping out of bounds
8. Glitching the game to achieve a positive outcome
9. Abusing quit-outs to skip required dialogue or actions, infinitely build damage, or infinitely acquire runes

The Shadow Keep **front-to-back skip** is also banned.

## 4.2 Specifically Banned Skips

The banned-skip list includes, but is not limited to:

- Noble Skip
- Radahn Stake Skip
- Using critical hits to cancel fall damage
- Using the explosive Physick to cancel fall damage
- Zips
- Wrong warping
- Front-to-back skip

## 4.3 Specifically Banned Cheeses

The banned-cheese list includes, but is not limited to:

- Knocking Draconic Tree Sentinel off a cliff
- Making the Dragonbarrow Night's Cavalry jump off a cliff
- Freezing Blue Loretta's AI
- Flying Dragon Greyll T-pose death
- Making Radahn comet off the map
- Quitting out to respawn Greyoll's baby dragons
- Riposting the Omenkiller or Dragonbarrow Bell Bearing Hunter off a cliff
- Running NPCs off cliffs
- Standing on the tree branch above Commander O'Neil and striking the tree with a metal weapon
- Using the Ghiza's Wheel machine-gun glitch against Rykard
- Serpent-Hunter moveset swaps
- Killing Devonia with the Furnace Golem

# 5. Squares

## 5.1 When a Square May Be Marked

A square may be marked only after every listed requirement has been completed.

| Requirement | Completion point |
| --- | --- |
| Boss or invader | “Enemy Felled,” equivalent text, and its accompanying sound |
| Enemy without victory text | Rune acquisition |
| Item | HUD notification or accompanying sound |
| Sacred Flask upgrade | Upgrade confirmed in the menu |
| Stat requirement | Confirmed in the level-up screen |
| Cave, tunnel, catacomb, or similar dungeon | Main boss defeated |
| Purchased item | Purchase completed |
| Weapon or Spirit Ash upgrade | Upgrade purchased |
| Great Rune restoration | “Great Rune Restored” appears |
| Imbibe at St. Trina | Screen fades to black |
| Transformation | Transformation status indicators appear |
| Return Latenna to the apostate | Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone received |

## 5.2 Squares Marked Early

Players may not mark squares early. An accidentally early mark has a **10-second grace period** in which it must be removed.

A square is officially marked early if it remains incorrectly marked for more than 10 seconds. The offending player is locked out of that square, and the opponent is informed.

Regardless of the grace period, if an early mark prevents an opponent from claiming a square the opponent otherwise would have won, the offending player must forfeit that square to the opponent.

## 5.3 Squares Marked Late

Players must mark a completed square within **30 seconds**. A mark made after that period is officially late. The offending player is locked out of that square, and the opponent is informed.

## 5.4 Unmarked Squares

Every completed square must be marked within 30 seconds. If the player does not mark it before the grace period ends and before referee intervention, the square is officially unmarked.

The offending player is locked out of an officially unmarked square. The opponent is not informed of this infraction.

## 5.5 Grace-Period Abuse

Grace periods exist for honest mistakes and may not be used to gain an unfair advantage. Deliberately marking early or late for an advantage is illegal even if the action occurs inside the applicable grace period.

A first finding results in a stern warning from referees or league organizers. Continued abuse may be punished at the league's discretion. Decisions about grace-period abuse are not disputable.

# 6. Crashes and Glitches

## 6.1 Player Crashes

Crashes are an unavoidable risk of modded Elden Ring. A player whose game, computer, or stream crashes must make restoring it the immediate priority, even when that requires abandoning an in-progress game action or boss attempt.

- A simple game crash does not stop the match.
- A severe PC or stream crash pauses the match.
- Immediately notify the referee, or the opponent when no referee is present, if a pause is needed.
- Keep a phone or secondary device nearby in case the PC cannot be used for communication.
- The match timer is paused once league organizers know about the crash.
- Notify the officials when ready to resume.

Referees will attempt to alert the opponent and limit any advantage caused by the crash.

## 6.2 Opponent Crashes

When an opponent crashes, the other player must take reasonable steps to limit any advantage. The player will be asked to quit out at the earliest reasonable moment and must quit immediately when notified by the referee.

## 6.3 EldenBingo Application Problems

Application problems may include opponent marks not updating, inability to mark a square, or the match timer freezing.

1. Restart EldenBingo and rejoin the match lobby.
2. If the problem continues, quit out of Elden Ring and immediately notify the referee.
3. The opponent will be asked to quit at the earliest reasonable time.
4. The match timer will be paused.
5. Troubleshoot with referees and league organizers. A PC restart may be required in severe cases.

## 6.4 Game Glitches

Intentionally glitching the game is prohibited.

An accidental glitch that can be corrected, including wrong warping, Torrent hover, item duplication, or zipping, must be corrected immediately. If an accidental glitch cannot be corrected, such as an enemy unintentionally jumping from a cliff or its AI freezing, play may continue normally. Referees and league organizers may investigate any glitch.

If a glitch prevents normal play, first attempt to correct it by quitting out or restarting Elden Ring. If the problem remains, quit the game and immediately notify the referee. The opponent will be asked to quit at the earliest reasonable moment, and the timer will be paused while officials troubleshoot. A PC restart may be required in severe cases.

Streamed matches use a temporary Discord with four voice channels:

- Commentary team
- Private channel for the first player

- Referees

At match start, enter the commentary channel so organizers can confirm everyone is ready. Once confirmed, move to the assigned private channel.

Players may mute their microphones but may not deafen during a match.

During a match, only the assigned referees should contact a player. Referee instructions are binding and must be followed. Failure to cooperate may result in a penalty or disqualification.

# 8. Banned Actions and Resources

## 8.1 Macros and Scripts

Pre-recorded macros and scripts are prohibited. Every input must be performed individually by the player through the player's chosen input device.

Prohibited examples include, but are not limited to:

- Fast quit-out macros
- Automatic dodges
- Automatic parries
- Moveset-swap scripts
- Movement scripts

## 8.2 Unapproved Mods

Only the modifications listed in Section 2.1 are allowed. The approved game modifications are the Elden Ring Bingo Randomizer, SkipTheIntro, and ERTool when used for stutter fixes and avoiding achievement bugs or freezing.

All other game modifications are banned. Players using accessibility modifications must contact HooZher directly through Discord.

## 8.3 Outside Help and Communication

Players may not receive outside help or communication during matches through Twitch chat, Discord, or any other source. Twitch chat requirements are described in Section 2.3.

# 9. Penalties

## 9.1 Time Penalties

When referees are present, they may issue a time penalty approximately twice the time advantage gained. The penalized player must quit out for the assigned duration.

Reasons include, but are not limited to:

- Controlling the character before the match begins
- Refusing to quit when instructed by a referee
- Resuming a paused match before being told to resume

## 9.2 Square Penalties

When referees are present, they may require a player to unmark or forfeit a square or may lock the player out of a square.

An in-game action completed through an illegal skip or cheese does not count toward any board square.

## 9.3 Position Penalties

When referees are present, they may issue position penalties to remove an illegal advantage. A player receiving a position penalty must use the **Memory of Grace** immediately.

Reasons include, but are not limited to:

- Using an illegal skip
- Using an illegal cheese
- Deliberately using glitches

# 10. Code of Conduct

Elden Squares is a league for Elden Ring Bingo enthusiasts of every skill level. It prioritizes fun, inclusion, and friendly competition.

Players are expected to demonstrate good sportsmanship and positive communication. By competing, players represent the best the community has to offer and are encouraged to grow, improve, and contribute positively to the grassroots Elden Squares Bingo community.

Players are also responsible for promoting positive conduct within their own communities. Abuse by a player or the player's community will not be tolerated.

If a player is unable to schedule or arrive on time for two Ignite league matches within six months, HooZher may remove that player from current and future Elden Squares events.
