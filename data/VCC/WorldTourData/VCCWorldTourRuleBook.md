# VCC World Tour Rulebook

**Version 1.0**

This rulebook governs the VCC World Tour competition. It covers league structure, match setup, board selection, competitive rules, square completion, technical issues, communication, penalties, player conduct, and liability.

> **Authority:** The VCC World Tour is hosted in IGNITE by Shura and Thomas, who hold executive authority over the league and this rulebook. Event administrators and assigned referees may issue binding match rulings under that authority.

# 1. World Tour Overview

  

## 1.1 Competition Path

  

The VCC World Tour is played through a group stage, a Play-In Stage, and a seeded playoff bracket. Match creation, scheduling, board drafts, qualification status, and playoff slots are managed through the VCC World Tour portal.

  

## 1.2 Group Stage

  

The group stage lasts two weeks, and there are two pools of groups.

  
Pool 1 - 3 groups:
- **Ancestral Followers, Banished Knights, and Carian Royals:** Each group contains four teams. Every team plays the other three teams in its division once.

Pool 2 - 1 group:
- **Challenger Division:** Six teams each play four games against four different opponents. Every team skips one of the other five teams.

  

Standings are decided in this order unless an event ruling announces otherwise:

  

1. Match record

2. Head-to-head result when applicable

3. Strength of victory

4. Bingos

  

## 1.3 Play-In Stage and Last-Chance Qualifier

  

The Play-In Stage lasts one week.

  
For the advanced divisions:

- **The three first-place teams** play a round robin to determine Seeds 1-3. All three teams retain their playoff byes regardless of their play-in results.

- **The three runners-up** play a round robin. The winner earns Seed 4 and the fourth playoff bye; the other two teams qualify for the Round of 12 as Seeds 5-6, ordered by result.

- **The three third-place teams** play a round robin to determine Seeds 7-9. All three teams qualify for the Round of 12 regardless of their play-in results.

For the Last-Chance Qualifier:

- **The fourth-place team** from each advanced division joins the top three Challenger Division teams in the Last-Chance Qualifier. Match pairings will be randomized. The LCQ produces the final three playoff teams.

  

## 1.4 Playoff Seeding

  

The 12-team playoff field is seeded as follows:

  

1. Seeds 1-3: the winners of Ancestral Followers, Banished Knights, and Carian Royals, ordered by the first-place play-in results. All three receive a playoff bye.

2. Seed 4: the winner of the second-place Play-In.

3. Seeds 5-6: the two remaining Play-In teams, ordered by Play-In result.

4. Seeds 7-9: the third-place teams from the advanced divisions, ordered by the third-place play-in results.

5. Seeds 10-12: the three LCQ qualifiers, randomly ordered.

  

Round of 12 pairings should avoid group-stage rematches wherever reasonably possible. Event administrators manually assign playoff bracket slots as qualification results become final. An undecided slot may be represented by a **Placeholder/TBD team** until its qualifier is known.

  

## 1.5 Reporting Results

  

After a match, players must submit the required evidence through the World Tour match workspace or the results channel designated by event administrators. Evidence may include a completed-board screenshot, exported replay JSON, VOD link, and requested player-square totals. A result is not official until approved by an administrator.

  

## 1.6 Referees and Match Officials

  

Event administrators assign referees for featured, streamed, and other matches when necessary. Referee instructions are binding during play. When no referee is present, teams must document disputes immediately and preserve screenshots, VODs, replay files, and relevant communications for review.

  

## 1.7 Scheduling

  

Teams schedule through the World Tour player portal and their assigned communication channels.

  

- Players may propose and accept match times in the match workspace.

- A match may require final administrator approval before an accepted time becomes official.

- A team that does not arrive within 15 minutes of the official time may forfeit.

- Negligence that prevents scheduling may result in a loss for the responsible team. If both teams are negligent, both may receive a loss.

- Scheduling disputes must be reported to event administrators as soon as they occur.

# 2. Streaming and Setup

  

## 2.1 Required Mods and Tools

  

Matches are hosted through the **EldenBingo application**. Players must use the **Athena Randomizer** to randomize weapons and play on the most up-to-date Elden Ring patch.

  

If Elden Ring is patched during the season, league organizers will announce how matches should proceed.

  

Allowed tools and modifications include:

  

- ERTool for stutter fixes, avoiding achievement bugs or freezing, and skipping the intro

- SkipTheIntro

- Elden Ring Map Genie

- Fextralife

- Rune-level calculators

  

Players may not seek help from other people, other than their teammate/coach, including Twitch chat. 

  

## 2.2 Streaming Requirements

  

Commentated matches must be streamed live on Twitch.

  

- Confirm the participating Twitch channel in the player portal or with the assigned broadcast administrator before the match.

- Contact an event administrator if streaming is a problem so an alternative can be arranged.

- Use the personalized OBS overlay URL supplied through the World Tour match workspace when requested.

- Players may place anything they want in the overlay margins.

- If using a facecam, align it with the indicated position so it can appear on the main broadcast.

  

## 2.3 Chat Moderation

  

Players may not receive spoilers, hints, or other match information from Twitch chat.

  

Streamers must ensure that moderators promptly remove prohibited information. If chat reveals match information, responsibility rests with the player or streamer. Receiving prohibited information may result in penalties, including match forfeiture.

# 3. Match Structure

  

## 3.1 Starting on Time



It is expected that pick-ban will begin at the scheduled time. A player who cannot begin because of a personal or technical issue must tell the opponent.

  

A player who does not arrive and provides no communication receives a 15-minute grace period. If the player has not arrived when that period ends, the player forfeits the match.

  

## 3.2 Five-Minute Prep and Warmup

  

Each match begins with a five-minute prep and warmup period. Players may see the selected board during this period but may not control their characters.

  

Before the warmup begins, players or referees confirm that the weapon randomizer works correctly. Players may not inspect a starting class other than Vagabond before this confirmation.

  

During the warmup, players may:

  

- Select a starting class (or multiple) and keepsake

- Check Roundtable Hold

- Purchase items

- Perform other setup that does not leave Roundtable Hold or begin Fia's quest

  

Players may leave Roundtable Hold or begin Fia's quest once the timer reaches zero.

  

A false start results in a time penalty approximately twice the advantage gained. When a referee is present, the referee determines the official penalty length.

  

## 3.3 Match Duration

  

The official match timer begins when the warmup ends. A match lasts no more than two hours. If no victory condition is met within two hours, the match ends on points or proceeds to overtime under the victory rules below.

  

## 3.4 Victory Conditions

  

Every match must have a winner.

  

- The first team to **13 points** wins.

- Each marked square is worth **1 point**.

- Each completed Bingo line is worth **2 points**.

  

At two hours, a tied match enters up to 15 minutes of sudden-death overtime. The first team to mark a square wins. If neither team marks a square, the team that first reached the tying score wins.

# 4. Pick/Ban System

  

## 4.1 Purpose and Board Pool

  

Each independent set begins with a match-specific pool of **three boards** assigned by event administration. The pick/ban process removes two boards and leaves one decider board for play. Submissions are private so neither team can react to the opponent's choice.

  

## 4.2 When Boards Become Available

  

The three-board pool remains hidden until the official scheduled match time. Once the Board Draft tab opens in the player portal, each team reviews the available boards and submits its choices. Teams may discuss choices only with eligible teammates and coaches under the communication rules.

  

## 4.3 Private Submissions

  

1. **Team A** selects one primary ban.

2. **Team B** selects one primary ban and one different fallback ban.

3. Each submission remains hidden until both teams have submitted.

4. The portal resolves both submissions simultaneously and reveals the two banned boards and the remaining decider.

  

Team B's fallback resolves a collision when both teams choose the same primary board. Team A does not submit a fallback.

  

## 4.4 How the Draft Resolves

  

| Submission result | Resolution |

| --- | --- |

| Team A and Team B choose different primary bans | Both primary choices are banned. The third board becomes the decider. |

| Both teams choose the same primary ban | The shared primary choice and Team B's fallback are banned. The third board becomes the decider. |

  

**Example:** Boards 1, 2, and 3 are available. Team A bans Board 1. Team B submits Board 1 as its primary and Board 2 as its fallback. Because the primary bans collide, Boards 1 and 2 are removed and Board 3 is played.

  

## 4.5 Review, Submission, and Corrections

  

Before submitting, each team must review its displayed primary choice and, for Team B, fallback choice. Team B's fallback may not match its primary. Once choices are queued, they remain private and cannot be changed unless an administrator resets the draft because of a verified error or technical problem.

  

Players must not share, infer through unauthorized access, or attempt to reveal the opponent's queued choices. Exploiting the portal, browser tools, database access, stream information, or outside communication to learn a private choice is prohibited.

  

## 4.6 Decider Seed and Begin Match

  

After the draft resolves, the decider board is visible. Its seed remains hidden until the configured release time. Seeds release automatically after the match's draft window, or earlier when both teams confirm **Begin Match** in the portal. The default draft window is 30 minutes unless event administration configures a different period for that match.

  

Teams may not begin the timed run from a hidden or unreleased seed. If an administrator directs a reset, replacement board, or delayed release, that ruling controls.

  

## 4.7 Best-of-Three Sets

  

In a best-of-three matchup, every game is an independent best-of-one set with its own three-board pool and pick/ban. Completing one set does not carry bans, board priority, or seeds into the next set. Required participants must confirm readiness before the next set opens.

  

## 4.8 Pick/Ban Violations

  

Submitting for the wrong team, using unauthorized information, intentionally delaying the draft, beginning before seed release, or manipulating the application may result in a draft reset, board loss, time penalty, set loss, match forfeiture, or further discipline. Event administrators determine the remedy based on competitive impact.

# 5. Skips and Cheeses

  

## 5.1 General Standard

  

The league is intended to be played without major skips or cheeses. A skip or cheese is banned when it intentionally performs an action that is clearly unintended to a knowledgeable player and involves any of the following:

  

1. Gravity kills

2. Stake skips

3. Directly skipping an otherwise required boss

4. Breaking enemy AI so it has no meaningful opportunity to damage the player

5. Fall-damage canceling

6. Running bosses into kill planes

7. Clipping out of bounds

8. Glitching the game to achieve a positive outcome

9. Abusing quit-outs to skip required dialogue or actions, infinitely build damage, or infinitely acquire runes

  

The Shadow Keep **front-to-back skip** is also banned.

  

## 5.2 Specifically Banned Skips

  

The banned-skip list includes, but is not limited to:

  

- Noble Skip

- Radahn Stake Skip

- Using critical hits to cancel fall damage

- Using the explosive Physick to cancel fall damage

- Zips

- Wrong warping

- Front-to-back skip

  

## 5.3 Specifically Banned Cheeses

  

The banned-cheese list includes, but is not limited to:

  

- Knocking Draconic Tree Sentinel off a cliff

- Making the Dragonbarrow Night's Cavalry jump off a cliff

- Freezing Blue Loretta's AI

- Flying Dragon Greyll T-pose death

- Making Radahn comet off the map

- Quitting out to respawn Greyoll's baby dragons

- Riposting the Omenkiller or Dragonbarrow Bell Bearing Hunter off a cliff

- Running NPCs off cliffs

- Standing on the tree branch above Commander O'Neil and striking the tree with a metal weapon

- Using the Ghiza's Wheel machine-gun glitch against Rykard

- Serpent-Hunter moveset swaps

- Killing Devonia with the Furnace Golem

# 6. Squares

  

## 6.1 When a Square May Be Marked

  

A square may be marked only after every listed requirement has been completed.

  

| Requirement | Completion point |

| --- | --- |

| Boss or invader | “Enemy Felled,” equivalent text, and its accompanying sound |

| Enemy without victory text | Rune acquisition |

| Item | HUD notification or accompanying sound |

| Sacred Flask upgrade | Upgrade confirmed in the menu |

| Stat requirement | Confirmed in the level-up screen |

| Cave, tunnel, catacomb, or similar dungeon | Main boss defeated |

| Purchased item | Purchase completed |

| Weapon or Spirit Ash upgrade | Upgrade purchased |

| Great Rune restoration | “Great Rune Restored” appears |

| Imbibe at St. Trina | Screen fades to black |

| Transformation | Transformation status indicators appear |

| Return Latenna to the apostate | Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone received |

  

## 6.2 Squares Marked Early

  

Players may not mark squares early. An accidentally early mark has a **10-second grace period** in which it must be removed.

  

A square is officially marked early if it remains incorrectly marked for more than 10 seconds. The offending player is locked out of that square, and the opponent is informed.

  

Regardless of the grace period, if an early mark prevents an opponent from claiming a square the opponent otherwise would have won, the offending player must forfeit that square to the opponent.

  

## 6.3 Squares Marked Late

  

Players must mark a completed square within **30 seconds**. A mark made after that period is officially late. The offending player is locked out of that square, and the opponent is informed.

  

## 6.4 Unmarked Squares

  

Every completed square must be marked within 30 seconds. If the player does not mark it before the grace period ends and before referee intervention, the square is officially unmarked.

  

The offending player is locked out of an officially unmarked square. The opponent is not informed of this infraction.

  

## 6.5 Grace-Period Abuse

  

Grace periods exist for honest mistakes and may not be used to gain an unfair advantage. Deliberately marking early or late for an advantage is illegal even if the action occurs inside the applicable grace period.

  

A first finding results in a stern warning from referees or league organizers. Continued abuse may be punished at the league's discretion. Decisions about grace-period abuse are not disputable.

# 7. Crashes and Glitches

  

## 7.1 Player Crashes

  

Crashes are an unavoidable risk of modded Elden Ring. A player whose game, computer, or stream crashes must make restoring it the immediate priority, even when that requires abandoning an in-progress game action or boss attempt.

  

- A simple game crash does not stop the match.

- A severe PC or stream crash pauses the match.

- Immediately notify the referee, or the opponent when no referee is present, if a pause is needed.

- Keep a phone or secondary device nearby in case the PC cannot be used for communication.

- The match timer is paused once league organizers know about the crash.

- Notify the officials when ready to resume.

  

Referees will attempt to alert the opponent and limit any advantage caused by the crash.

  

## 7.2 Opponent Crashes

  

When an opponent crashes, the other player must take reasonable steps to limit any advantage. The player will be asked to quit out at the earliest reasonable moment and must quit immediately when notified by the referee.

  

## 7.3 EldenBingo Application Problems

  

Application problems may include opponent marks not updating, inability to mark a square, or the match timer freezing.

  

1. Restart EldenBingo and rejoin the match lobby.

2. If the problem continues, quit out of Elden Ring and immediately notify the referee.

3. The opponent will be asked to quit at the earliest reasonable time.

4. The match timer will be paused.

5. Troubleshoot with referees and league organizers. A PC restart may be required in severe cases.

  

## 7.4 Game Glitches

  

Intentionally glitching the game is prohibited.

  

An accidental glitch that can be corrected, including wrong warping, Torrent hover, item duplication, or zipping, must be corrected immediately. If an accidental glitch cannot be corrected, such as an enemy unintentionally jumping from a cliff or its AI freezing, play may continue normally. Referees and league organizers may investigate any glitch.

  

If a glitch prevents normal play, first attempt to correct it by quitting out or restarting Elden Ring. If the problem remains, quit the game and immediately notify the referee. The opponent will be asked to quit at the earliest reasonable moment, and the timer will be paused while officials troubleshoot. A PC restart may be required in severe cases.

Streamed matches use a temporary Discord with four voice channels:

  

- Commentary team

- Private channel for the first player

- Private channel for the second player

After the match, referees may invite players to an interview with the commentary team. Interviews are encouraged but optional. Enter the commentary channel and wait when accepting an interview. After interviews, opponents may speak together if both agree and time permits.

  

## 8.2 Referee Communication

  

During a match, only the assigned referees should contact a player. Referee instructions are binding and must be followed. Failure to cooperate may result in a penalty or disqualification.

# 9. Banned Actions and Resources

  

## 9.1 Macros and Scripts

  

Pre-recorded macros and scripts are prohibited. Every input must be performed individually by the player through the player's chosen input device.

  

Prohibited examples include, but are not limited to:

  

- Fast quit-out macros

- Automatic dodges

- Automatic parries

- Moveset-swap scripts

- Movement scripts

  

## 9.2 Unapproved Mods

  

Only the modifications listed in Section 2.1 are allowed. The approved game modifications are the Elden Ring Bingo Randomizer, SkipTheIntro, and ERTool when used for stutter fixes and avoiding achievement bugs or freezing.

  

All other game modifications are banned. Players using accessibility modifications must contact event administrators through Discord before competing.

  

## 9.3 Outside Help and Communication

  

Players may not receive outside help or communication during matches through Twitch chat, Discord, or any other source. Twitch chat requirements are described in Section 2.3.

# 10. Penalties

  

## 10.1 Time Penalties

  

When referees are present, they may issue a time penalty approximately twice the time advantage gained. The penalized player must quit out for the assigned duration.

  

Reasons include, but are not limited to:

  

- Controlling the character before the match begins

- Refusing to quit when instructed by a referee

- Resuming a paused match before being told to resume

  

## 10.2 Square Penalties

  

When referees are present, they may require a player to unmark or forfeit a square or may lock the player out of a square.

  

An in-game action completed through an illegal skip or cheese does not count toward any board square.

  

## 10.3 Position Penalties

  

When referees are present, they may issue position penalties to remove an illegal advantage. A player receiving a position penalty must use the **Memory of Grace** immediately.

  

Reasons include, but are not limited to:

  

- Using an illegal skip

- Using an illegal cheese

- Deliberately using glitches

# 11. Code of Conduct

  

Elden Squares and the VCC World Tour is a league for Elden Ring Bingo enthusiasts of every skill level. It prioritizes fun, inclusion, and friendly competition.

  

Players are expected to demonstrate good sportsmanship and positive communication. By competing, players represent the best the community has to offer and are encouraged to grow, improve, and contribute positively to the grassroots Elden Squares Bingo community.

  

Players are also responsible for promoting positive conduct within their own communities. Abuse by a player or the player's community will not be tolerated.

  

Repeated scheduling failures or no-shows may result in removal from the current World Tour and future Elden Squares events at organizer discretion.
