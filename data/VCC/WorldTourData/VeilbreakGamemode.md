# Veilbreak Gamemode

## VCC World Tour Player Guide

Veilbreak is Ignite's competitive **2v2 Base Game + DLC Bingo format**, played with the **Athena Randomizer**.

The format opens more of Elden Ring from the beginning of a run and introduces structured progression unlocks throughout the base game and *Shadow of the Erdtree*. These changes make full-game routing practical while preserving meaningful decisions around bosses, regions, equipment, and travel.

This guide explains the Veilbreak ruleset, starting state, randomized grace pools, progression triggers, access requirements, shop changes, and other modified game logic.

# 4. Base-Game Progression Unlocks

Veilbreak uses progression triggers to open distant routes without removing the decisions required to reach those triggers.

## Dectus Progression

Picking up one Dectus Medallion half unlocks the grace at the **opposite fort**:

- Picking up **Dectus Left** unlocks **Fort Faroth**.
- Picking up **Dectus Right** unlocks **Fort Haight**.

This is a **one-directional trigger**. Picking up the second half later does not retroactively grant the grace at the first fort.

### Example

If a player collects the medallion half at Fort Haight first, Fort Faroth is unlocked. If the player later collects the half at Fort Faroth, Fort Haight is not then awarded. Players should activate the grace at the first fort they visit if they expect to need it.

## Altus Progression

The first time a player activates a qualifying Altus progression grace, the player receives:

- Outer Wall Battleground
- Road of Iniquity
- Craftsman's Shack
- Snow Valley Ruins Overlook
- Inner Consecrated Snowfield
- Nameless Eternal City
- Lake of Rot Shoreside
- **3 Sacred Tears**

### Qualifying Altus Graces

- Altus Plateau
- Erdtree-Gazing Hill
- Abandoned Coffin
- Altus Highway Junction
- Seethewater River
- Shaded Castle Ramparts
- Shaded Castle Inner Gate
- Outer Wall Phantom Tree
- Outer Wall Battleground
- Forest-Spanning Greatbridge
- Bower of Bounty

## Haligtree Access

- The **Ordina puzzle portal barrier** is removed.
- The evergaol puzzle remains available, but it no longer blocks progression through the portal.
- Touching **Haligtree Canopy** unlocks **Elphael Inner Wall**.

## Mohgwyn Palace Access

The Consecrated Snowfield Mohgwyn warp uses the following current behavior:

1. Touch the **Sword Monument at Rose Church** in Liurnia.
2. Wait for the unlock message to appear. The player may warp away after seeing it.
3. At the Consecrated Snowfield portal, defeat or despawn the **Sanguine Noble invader**.
4. Activate the portal.

The portal currently sends the player to its **vanilla destination** rather than the Lord of Blood's Favor / Dynasty Medallion destination.

## Crumbling Farum Azula Access

- Touching **Foot of the Forge** unlocks **Crumbling Beast Grave**.
- Touching **Dragon Temple** unlocks:
  - Dragon Temple Rooftop
  - Beside the Great Bridge
  - Ruins of the First Lord

## Radahn Defeat Unlock

Defeating **Starscourge Radahn** unlocks **War-Dead Catacombs**.

## Academy Abduction

Academy Abduction behaves like Randomizer logic. A player may jump to the bottom of the Raya Lucaria water wheel and die to complete the transition.

## Fia / Fortissax Progression

After giving D the **Weathered Dagger**, progressing to D's body grants **Price of Death's Throne** in Deeproot Depths.

## Ashen Capital Access

Restoring any Great Rune—except **Rennala's Great Rune of the Unborn**—grants the **Queen's Bedchamber** grace for Ashen Capital. **Malenia's Great Rune** can be restored without going through Leyndell. One can take the Weeping trap chest and take that warp once Malenia is dead.

- Ashen Capital functions as a **pocket dimension**.
- Entering it does not alter the outside world or the normal Leyndell, Royal Capital state.
- The player can access **Gideon**, **Godfrey / Hoarah Loux**, and **Radagon / Elden Beast**.
- Defeating **Godfrey / Hoarah Loux** grants access to the DLC.

# 5. DLC Access and Progression

## Unlocking the DLC

A player gains DLC access by satisfying any one of the following routes.

### Route A: Defeat Any 2 Early Remembrance Bosses

- Godrick the Grafted
- Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon
- Starscourge Radahn
- Regal Ancestor Spirit
- Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy
- Astel, Naturalborn of the Void

### Route B: Defeat Any 1 Advanced Remembrance Boss

- Lichdragon Fortissax
- Mohg, Lord of Blood
- Malenia, Blade of Miquella
- Fire Giant
- Morgott, the Omen King
- Maliketh, the Black Blade
- Dragonlord Placidusax

### Route C: Defeat Godfrey in Ashen Capital

Godfrey is available through Veilbreak's Ashen Capital pocket dimension. Defeating him also grants DLC access.

## DLC Entry Rewards

When DLC access is granted, the player receives:

- Scorched Ruins
- Moorth Ruins
- Manse Hall
- **5 Golden Seeds**
- **5 Sacred Tears**

## Ancient Dragon-Man Progression

Defeating **Ancient Dragon-Man** unlocks:

- Charo's Hidden Grave
- Foot of the Jagged Peak
- The Fissure

## Enir-Ilim Access

After defeating **Messmer** and **Romina**, burning the **Sealing Tree** unlocks **Divine Gate Front Staircase**.

## Promised Consort Radahn Fight

Entering the Promised Consort Radahn fight grants a temporary **+5 Scadutree Blessing** bonus.

- The bonus applies only while the character is in the fight.
- The bonus is removed when the character dies.
- The bonus is also removed when Promised Consort Radahn dies.

# 6. Global Game-Logic Changes

The following rules are active throughout Veilbreak:

- **Leyndell, Royal Capital** is unlocked from the start.
- The **Radahn Festival** is active from the start.
- Both **Ancestor Spirit** fights require only **1 brazier**.
- **Metyr, Mother of Fingers** is accessible after blowing **1 horn**.
- **Torrent can be ridden** inside the following boss arenas:
  - Astel, Naturalborn of the Void
  - Regal Ancestor Spirit
  - Ancestor Spirit
- The Roundtable Hold fist check is removed.
- Every class begins with **20 Vigor**.
- Rain is removed.
- Golden Eyes is removed.

# 7. Item and Merchant Adjustments

- **Pidia** sells the **Magic Scorpion Charm** for **5,000 runes**.
- The **Hermit Altus merchant** sells the **Sentry's Torch**; it is not randomized.
- The **Dragonbarrow merchant** sells the **Beast-Repellent Torch**; it is not randomized.
- **Sword of Light**, **Sword of Darkness**, and **Stone-Sheathed Sword** are not randomized.
- DLC perfume bottles are randomized only within the DLC.
- Buying a talisman from Bernie Bingo removes its overworld counterpart. (Except for the Drake Talismans)

# 9. Before Starting a Match

Players should confirm all of the following before beginning an official Veilbreak board:

- The current VCC World Tour squareset is being used.
- The required version of the Bingo App is available.
- The required version of the Athena Randomizer is installed.
- Both teams are using the same Veilbreak rules and mod version.
- Starting graces, runes, Vigor, and Vagabond is the same.
- The board seed and match seed match the official match workspace.
